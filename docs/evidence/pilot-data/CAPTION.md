# Figure caption

**Figure 1 | Synthetic repeated-measures data.** (a) Mean responses over time for Control and Treatment groups, with pointwise two-sided 95% Student-t confidence intervals (error bars and shading). Each group comprises three independent subjects measured at all four time points; intervals use the between-subject sample SD separately at each time point. Lines connect observed time points as visual guides. (b) Individual responses at the final time point (time = 3), with boxes showing the interquartile range, a median line, and whiskers extending to the most extreme values within 1.5 interquartile ranges. All observations are shown with horizontal jitter. No observations were excluded and no hypothesis tests were performed. Time and response units were not supplied.
