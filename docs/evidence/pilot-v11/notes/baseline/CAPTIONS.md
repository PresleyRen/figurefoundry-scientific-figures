## figure-1

Figure 1. Synthetic learning trajectories for Baseline, Context and Proposed. The supplied demonstration table contains six independent synthetic runs per method, observed at epochs 0, 5, 10, 15, 20, 25 and 30. Points and lines show the arithmetic mean at each observed epoch; vertical error bars and shaded bands show plus or minus one sample standard deviation (n−1 denominator) across the six runs. The same runs are followed over time, so epochs are repeated observations, not additional independent samples. Straight segments join observed mean values; no smoothing, interpolation of missing observations, exclusion, significance test or normalization was performed. Baseline is gray with circles and dashed lines, Context orange with triangles and dotted lines, and Proposed blue with squares and solid lines. The vertical axis is 40–85%; small horizontal padding retains complete endpoint markers. These Synthetic data illustrate figure production and do not establish real experimental superiority.
